# Business Brain OS — Client Onboarding Intake

Send this to the client before the build day, or work through it together on the onboarding call.

The system can't write in their voice, for their audience, about their offers until these answers exist. The more complete this is, the better everything works from day one.

---

## 1. Brand Voice

- [ ] Paste 5–10 examples of their own writing. Not their most polished work — the most natural. Emails, social posts, website copy, voice notes transcribed. Anything they wrote themselves.
- [ ] 3 words they'd use to describe their brand voice
- [ ] Any words or phrases they hate or would never say (e.g. "hustle", "crush it", "leverage")
- [ ] Their website URL

---

## 2. Ideal Customer

- [ ] Who is their customer? (role, situation, what stage of life or business they're in)
- [ ] What's the biggest problem they solve for them?
- [ ] 3–5 phrases their customers actually use — from DMs, testimonials, intake forms, or discovery calls. Exact wording if possible.
- [ ] What does their customer want most? (In the customer's words if possible)
- [ ] What does their customer fear most about making a change or buying something?

---

## 3. Offers

For each offer they sell:

- [ ] Name and price
- [ ] One-sentence description of what it is
- [ ] Who it's for (type of person and situation)
- [ ] The outcome — what changes for the customer after they buy?
- [ ] The most common objection before buying
- [ ] One real client result or success story

---

## 4. Tech Stack

- [ ] WordPress URL (if they publish blog content or landing pages)
- [ ] WordPress admin login (for the publishing scripts)
- [ ] Go High Level? Yes / No — if yes, their sub-account URL
- [ ] Which social platforms they post to (Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, Threads, etc.)
- [ ] Airtable? Yes / No — if yes, Cathy will set up their content tracking base

---

## 5. Tech Setup

Do these before the build day:

- [ ] Install Claude Code desktop app and sign in with their Claude account (claude.ai/download)
- [ ] Create a free GitHub account at github.com — share their username with Cathy so she can add them to the repo
- [ ] Install GitHub Desktop from desktop.github.com
- [ ] Any MCPs they want connected — Cathy sets these up on the build day (Airtable, Google Drive, Canva, etc.)

---

## 6. Content Goals

- [ ] What formats they want to produce (blog posts, emails, social posts, video scripts, etc.)
- [ ] How often they want to post (daily, a few times a week, weekly)
- [ ] Any existing content library — old blogs, testimonials, case studies they want to feed in

---

## 7. Existing Assets (bring to the build day)

- [ ] Logo files (colour version + white/reversed version if they have one)
- [ ] Brand colours (hex codes if known)
- [ ] Any headshots or photos they use in their marketing
- [ ] Their best testimonials — 5–10 is plenty to start
