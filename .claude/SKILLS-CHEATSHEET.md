# Business Brain OS — Skills Cheatsheet

A quick reference to every skill in your system. Keep this open while you work.
To use a skill, just describe what you want in plain English — Claude will route to the right one automatically.

---

## Start Here — Onboarding (run once)

| Skill | What it does | Say something like... |
|---|---|---|
| `setup-user-profile` | Asks you questions and writes your USER.md — who you are, your brand, your working style | "Run setup-user-profile" |
| `setup-north-star` | Asks questions and writes your CEO north star and revenue tracker | "Run setup-north-star" |
| `setup-brand-voice` | Guides you through adding writing samples, then generates your voice profile | "Run setup-brand-voice" |
| `setup-icp` | Interviews you and writes your ideal customer profile and audience language file | "Run setup-icp" |
