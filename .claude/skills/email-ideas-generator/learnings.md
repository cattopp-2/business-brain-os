### 2026-05-18 — birthday email rewrite comparison
- **Paragraph breaks**: One or two sentences per paragraph max. Much more aggressive than AI defaults.
- **Include client work**: When listing what Cathy got done, include actual client tasks — not just her own content. Makes it feel like a real working day.
- **Soft blog CTAs**: "if you fancy a read" rather than "read it here" or presenting the blog as a definitive resource. Warmer, less directive.
- **Self-referential humour**: The meta joke about the humaniser ("I didn't have to edit out any dashes in this email...IFYKY") is exactly right for nurture emails. Lean into it when the context allows.
- **Parenthetical warmth**: "(no kiddos, just us two (like the good old days))" — specific personal detail in brackets adds warmth without slowing the email down.
- **Skip the URL in the email body**: Cathy didn't include the raw URL inline — just a mention and light CTA. The link goes in as a hyperlink or on its own line, not embedded in a sentence.
