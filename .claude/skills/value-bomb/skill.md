# Value Bomb

A one-sentence (or short) Facebook post that offers a freebie via comment. The comment is the conversion event. The DM is where the real work happens.

## The Formula

> "This [specific tactic/method/system/template] helps my clients [specific measurable result] in just [24/48 hours]. Want [the asset]?"

**Key rules:**
- The result must be specific and measurable — a number, not a vague outcome
- The timeframe must feel fast: 24 or 48 hours
- The asset is small — something consumable in under 10 minutes
- Phantom bomb: if the asset doesn't exist yet, post anyway and only create it when people ask
- No URLs, no landing pages — the comment is the conversion event
- 90% of the time what they ask for is not what they actually need — the value bomb intercepts the conversation, it doesn't end it

---

## Intake — Ask Before Writing

Ask one at a time:

1. What result does Cathy's tactic, tool, or system produce? (needs a specific number + outcome)
2. What timeframe — 24 or 48 hours?
3. What asset is she offering? (checklist, swipe file, template, prompt sequence) — or "phantom" if she'll create it on demand
4. Which offer should this conversation eventually lead toward? (shapes the DM direction)

## CTA Variations

Choose based on what feels natural for this post:

- "Want it?"
- "Want the step-by-step guide?"
- "Want the swipe file and the 5-minute setup?"
- "Drop '[keyword]' below and it's yours."
- "Comment '[keyword]' and I'll send it over."

## After Comments Come In — DM Conversation Framework (WWLD)

The value bomb is the conversation opener, not the destination. The DM is where you diagnose the real problem.

**The guiding question for every conversation:** "What small win or clarity moment can I give this person in the next 10 minutes?"

If the value bomb doesn't fit their actual situation, pivot to one that does. If you don't have one, open a Google Doc and make one.

---

**Step 1 — Tell them why you're messaging (builds Clarity)**

Give specific options to choose from. Never ask open-ended questions.

> "Hey [name]! Messaging you because you asked for [value bomb]. I've got a couple of different options depending on where you're at. Tell me which fits best:
> - [Option A — low/no progress on the problem]
> - [Option B — some progress]
> - [Option C — active/further along]"

---

**Step 2 — Give them time to answer.**

---

**Step 3 — Diagnose the real problem (builds Certainty)**

Act as if they've just paid £500 to solve this. Ask for something specific based on their answer.

> "Can you show me [the last 3 posts / your current funnel / a link to your page]? I'll do a quick audit and give you a couple of tweaks that'll help. Does that sound good?"

---

**Step 4 — Audit and send a Loom**

Short, specific, actionable. Walk through what you found and what to fix.

---

**Step 5 — Propose a call**

> "There's one thing I'd need 15 minutes to walk you through properly. Would that be worth a quick call?"

---

**Step 6 — On the call**

Give them the full roadmap — the step-by-step plan to solve their problem on their own. Your service is the accelerated route with you navigating.
