---
name: seo-page-rewriter
description: Takes an existing written page and a target keyword, suggests the SEO edits needed to optimise it, then rewrites the full page once the suggestions are agreed. Use when a page has already been written and needs to be optimised for search — not for writing pages from scratch.
---

<objective>
Review an existing page against SEO best practices for a specific target keyword, present a clear list of suggested changes, then rewrite the full page with those changes applied once confirmed.

Two steps: suggestions first, rewrite second. Nothing is changed without agreement.
</objective>

<quick_start>
Paste the page content and confirm the target keyword. The skill will review it, suggest specific changes, and wait for your go-ahead before rewriting.
</quick_start>

<workflow>

**Step 1: Intake**

Ask for:
1. The target keyword for this page
2. The full page content — paste it in, including meta title, meta description, headings, and body copy (whatever they have)

If either is missing, ask before continuing.

---

**Step 2: SEO Review**

Review the page against the target keyword. Check each of the following elements and note what needs to change and why:

**Meta title**
- Is the keyword present?
- Is it 60 characters or under?
- Does it give a clear reason to click — not just a label?

**Meta description**
- Is the keyword present?
- Is it 160 characters or under?
- Does it read as a reason to click, not just a summary?

**H1**
- Is the keyword present?
- Is it benefit-led or outcome-focused — not just a service name?

**Headings (H2s)**
- Do they follow a logical reading order?
- Does at least one include the keyword or a closely related term?
- Do they cover the topic thoroughly enough?

**Opening paragraph**
- Does the keyword appear naturally in the first one or two sentences?

**Body copy**
- Is the keyword used naturally throughout — not forced or repeated too often?
- Are there related terms and phrases that reinforce the topic?
- Is the content specific and useful, or does it stay vague?

**CTAs**
- Is there at least one clear call to action?
- Is the next step obvious?

**Content length**
- Is there enough content to be genuinely useful to a reader? (Under 300 words is usually too thin for a service page.)

---

**Step 3: Present the Suggestions**

Present findings clearly. For each element that needs changing, give:
- What the issue is
- A specific suggested fix (write the replacement, don't just describe it)

Format:

---

**SEO Review: [page title or URL if known]**
*Target keyword: [keyword]*

**What's already working:**
[Brief list — elements that are fine as they are]

**Suggested changes:**

| Element | Issue | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|
| Meta title | Keyword missing, currently 74 characters | "[suggested replacement — max 60 chars]" |
| H1 | Reads as a label, not a benefit | "[suggested replacement]" |
| Opening paragraph | Keyword doesn't appear until paragraph 3 | Move keyword into the first sentence — suggested rewrite below |
| [etc.] | | |

*Where a full rewrite of a section is needed, show it in full below the table.*

---

Then ask:

> "Happy with these suggestions? Say yes to go ahead with the full rewrite, or let me know what you'd like to adjust first."

Wait for confirmation before proceeding.

---

**Step 4: Rewrite the Page**

Apply all agreed changes and output the complete rewritten page, formatted for easy copy-paste into a CMS:

---

**META TITLE:** [rewritten title]
**META DESCRIPTION:** [rewritten description]

# [H1]

[Rewritten body — full page, all sections, in order]

---

Keep the client's original structure, tone, and content where it doesn't need to change. Only rewrite what was flagged and agreed. Do not add new sections or change the purpose of the page.

</workflow>

<success_criteria>
Complete when:
- [ ] Target keyword and page content both collected
- [ ] Every SEO element reviewed and findings presented clearly
- [ ] Specific suggested rewrites provided (not just descriptions of problems)
- [ ] User has confirmed which suggestions to apply
- [ ] Full rewritten page delivered, ready to copy into a CMS
</success_criteria>
