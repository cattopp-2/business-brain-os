## Membership-Specific Writing Notes
- Copy sells on ongoing value, low commitment risk, and the cost of staying stuck
- "Every month" value must feel concrete and immediate, not vague
- Reduce perceived risk of joining — this is a recurring commitment, not a one-off purchase
- Frame cancellation policy with confidence and transparency, not apology
