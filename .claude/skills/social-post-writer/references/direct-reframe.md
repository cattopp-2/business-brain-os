# Framework: Direct Reframe

Opens with a sharp, specific challenge to a common belief or pattern. Names something clearly that most people feel but haven't articulated. The reframe arrives as a recognition, not as advice. Low-pressure CTA if there is one.

Different from Show Don't Tell (which teaches a method) — the Direct Reframe doesn't explain or instruct. It just names the thing clearly and lets it land.

## The Framework

1. Open with a sharp, specific challenge to a belief or pattern the audience holds
2. Name what's really happening underneath it — the thing they feel but haven't said
3. Build to the reframe — not as advice, as a recognition
4. Optional soft CTA (one line, low-pressure) — or none at all

## Writing Notes

- The opening line should be direct and specific, not a question
- Short paragraphs, but not every paragraph is one sentence — vary the rhythm deliberately
- The reframe should feel like relief, not a lesson
- No instruction, no "here's what to do instead" — the recognition is the point
- If there's a CTA, it should feel like a natural next step, not a pitch
