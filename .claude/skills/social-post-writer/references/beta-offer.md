# Framework: Beta Offer — Validate Early Results

For offers that have worked for you (and possibly a handful of clients) but you're not yet ready to make big promises because you don't have reliable group results. Being transparent about being in early stages builds trust. Limiting the group creates exclusivity and urgency.

## The Framework

Share your personal result and any early client evidence:
"I recently did X, which helped me with Y. I've tested it with a couple of clients and they got [specific result]."

Share the story behind it.

Be honest about where you are:
"I'm excited by the potential, but I need to test this a bit more."

Invite a small group of beta clients:
"My next step is to get a small group together (X people, max) to make sure these results are replicable."

Describe who you're looking for — be specific about the ideal client.

Offer a beta price that reflects you're still developing this:
"To reflect that we're still working this out, I'm offering the first [X] spots at [price]."

## Writing Notes

- Honesty is the point of this framework — don't oversell
- The beta price should feel genuinely fair given the early-stage context
- "Small group" is the urgency mechanism — keep the number real and small
- Avoid corporate language or false confidence

## Example

I recently created an audio hypnosis for myself to help with anxiety before speaking in front of a group. I shared it with a client who was delivering a guest expert session she was nervous about. She listened 30 minutes before the call and showed up feeling confident and energised — her words, not mine.

I'm excited by what I'm seeing, but I need to test this more widely before I can say this is reliably repeatable.

So my next step is to get a small group together — four of you, maximum — so I can refine the methodology and make sure these results hold.

If you struggle with visibility anxiety (putting yourself on video, podcasts, or stages), and you know you're holding back in your business because of it, I'd love to talk about you being a beta client for this intensive.

As I'm still ironing out the full approach, I'm offering these four spots at £97 each. I usually charge £800 for a month of 1:1 work — this reflects that we're building this together.

Interested? Drop me a message and let's have a chat.
