# Framework: Pain-Agitate-Solve

Cuts through the noise by starting exactly where the audience's frustration lives. Make them feel seen, crank the tension a little, then show how you fix it.

Works especially well for audiences who've already "tried things" and are frustrated by surface-level fixes. Positions the offer as the thing that finally makes sense.

## The Framework

**Pain** — Call out their specific struggle clearly and directly.

**Agitate** — Make the frustration feel more real. Name the emotional or practical consequences of staying stuck. Don't dramatise — just name the reality.

**Solve** — Position the offer as the specific, grounded solution. Not a vague promise — what you actually do and why it works.

## Writing Notes

- Name a specific, day-to-day struggle — not a vague category
- The agitate section should feel like recognition, not manipulation
- The solve should be concrete: what happens, how it works
- End with a single, clear CTA

## Example

**Problem**
You keep telling yourself you're fine.

You've got the tools, you've read the books, you've done the journaling, but your body still goes into overdrive at the smallest thing. Sleep is patchy and you're exhausted. Every time you try to "rest," your brain spins out.

**Agitate**
Your nervous system is stuck in a pattern it never learned to leave, and mindset work can only take you so far when your body still thinks it's in danger.

It's not a willpower problem or a discipline problem. It's about nervous system regulation — because if your body doesn't feel safe, everything else feels harder than it needs to be.
