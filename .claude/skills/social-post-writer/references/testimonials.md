# Framework: Testimonials

Leverages the power of social proof by sharing client results. Three variants depending on what you have available.

## Variant 1 — No Screenshot

Share the win, invite those who want similar results.

**Framework:**

[Share a specific win or result from a client]

Want to see if I can help you get similar results?

I'm currently working with / helping a few people [with the specific outcome].

[Who's a good fit — be specific]

Reach out if you'd like to have a chat.

---

## Variant 2 — Video Testimonial

Lead with the result, invite them to watch.

**Framework:**

1. Lead with the specific result from the video
2. Brief context — who they are and what they were dealing with
3. Invite them to watch
4. [Attach the video]

---

## Variant 3 — Written Testimonial / Screenshot

Lead with the result using a snippet from the testimonial, then summarise.

**Framework:**

1. Lead with the most impactful line from the testimonial (as a quote or paraphrase)
2. Summarise the transformation in your own words
3. Brief offer mention
4. CTA
5. [Attach the screenshot]

---

## Writing Notes

- Always lead with the result — not "I'm so proud of my client" but the actual outcome
- Attribute success to the client; Cathy's role is building the system or support structure
- Keep the post short and focused — the testimonial does the heavy lifting
- CTA should be low-pressure: "if this sounds like where you want to be, let's chat"
- Never manufacture urgency around testimonial posts — let the result speak

That's what this is supposed to feel like.
