# Framework: Breakthrough Moment

Shares a pivotal insight or discovery that led to significant change. Focuses on the moment everything shifted, then breaks down the understanding that made it possible. Different from the Epiphany framework — this is about a specific insight or strategy discovery, not a full origin story.

## The Framework

1. Lead with the breakthrough result
2. Share the key insight that made it possible
3. Explain how you apply this insight now
4. Show others how to implement it
5. Link to your offer for more breakthroughs
6. Clear CTA

## Writing Notes

- Open with the result — not "I've been thinking about..." but the actual change
- The insight should be specific and teachable, not vague inspiration
- "How to implement it" gives the audience real value before the pitch
- End with proof (results you or clients have seen) then the offer

## Example

Everything changed when I realised that managing money isn't about restriction — it's about alignment.

I remember the exact moment this hit me. I was reviewing my client Emma's fifth failed budget attempt. She'd tried everything: spreadsheets, cash envelopes, money apps. She was earning well but still living paycheck to paycheck, feeling guilty about every purchase.

As we dug deeper, something clicked. Every time Emma spent money in line with what she actually valued — health, family time, learning — she felt in control. But when she spent reactively or based on what she thought she "should" buy, the guilt and stress came flooding back.

That became the Values-Based Spending System. Instead of starting with numbers, we start with values.

Here's how it works. First, we identify your five core values — not the ones you think you should have, but what genuinely matters to you. Then we map your current spending against them. Emma discovered she was spending on clothes she rarely wore and subscriptions she barely used, while feeling guilty about family trips and art supplies that actually brought her joy. The third step is creating Money Alignment Zones — spending categories built around your values, not traditional budget lines.

Within three months, Emma's discretionary spending dropped 60%, her savings increased without feeling deprived, and her money anxiety virtually disappeared. She also started earning more — because when you feel aligned, you become more confident charging what you're worth.

This is now how I approach all money work with clients. Average savings increase of 30% within 90 days, significant reduction in money anxiety, and more confidence in financial decisions across the board.
