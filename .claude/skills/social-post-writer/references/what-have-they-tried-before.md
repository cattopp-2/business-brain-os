# Framework: What Have They Tried Before

Focuses on the personal journey of the ideal client — what they've specifically tried, why it didn't work for them, and what you're offering instead. Different from "Common Fix Making It Worse" which is about industry-wide trends. This one is about the individual's experience and frustration.

Respects their past efforts while offering hope for a better outcome.

## The Framework

1. Name the specific solutions this person has tried
2. Show you understand why they tried them
3. Explain why those approaches didn't solve the actual problem
4. Introduce your different approach and why it works
5. CTA

## Writing Notes

- "What they've tried" should feel like you're describing their specific experience, not making generalisations
- Be respectful — they tried those things for good reasons
- The "why it didn't work" section should name the root cause, not just say "it's not enough"
- Your solution should feel like a logical next step given the gap you've just named
