# Framework: Benefits Behind the Features

Highlights a specific feature of the offer, then immediately connects it to the tangible benefit or outcome it delivers. Bridges the gap between what you offer and what clients actually want.

Good for spotlighting a lesser-known element of an offer, or when the audience needs to understand why a specific thing matters to them.

## The Framework

1. Lead with a specific feature of your offer
2. Share the outcome or benefit of that feature
3. Explain the detail — how it works, what happens
4. CTA

## Writing Notes

- Lead with the feature plainly — name it clearly before explaining it
- The benefit statement should be specific and outcome-focused, not vague
- The detail section is where trust is built — specifics, not generalities
- One feature per post — don't try to cover everything
- CTA should invite the next logical step
