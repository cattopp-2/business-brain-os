# Framework: The Qualifier

A short, direct post that clearly outlines who you're looking to work with and what they need to bring to the table. Helps the right people self-identify and wrong fits opt out — which saves time for everyone.

## The Framework

I'm looking to work with [ideal client] who [has the specific problem you solve].

You've got to:
- [What they need to have in place]
- [What they need to have in place]
- [What they need to have in place]

[CTA]

## Writing Notes

- Be genuinely specific about the ideal client — not a broad category
- The "you've got to" list should be honest requirements, not aspirational fluff
- Keep the list to 3-4 items maximum
- The CTA should be low-pressure and conversational
- This works best when Cathy is actively filling spots for a specific offer

## Examples

**Example 1: Web Designer**

I'm looking to work with small business owners who have an outdated website that isn't converting.

You've got to:

- Have a clear understanding of your target audience
- Be ready to provide the content for your new site
- Have a budget of at least £3,000 for a complete overhaul

Ready to sort your online presence? Let's chat.

---

**Example 2: Health Coach**

I'm looking to work with busy professionals who want to lose weight and have more energy, without extreme diets.

You've got to:

- Be willing to make gradual changes rather than looking for a quick fix
- Have at least three hours a week to dedicate to meal prep and movement
- Be open to changing some long-standing habits

Ready to feel like yourself again? Let's connect.
