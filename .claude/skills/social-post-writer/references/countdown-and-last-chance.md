# Framework: Countdown / Last Chance

Two variants for end-of-launch urgency. Use when a genuine deadline is approaching. Never manufacture urgency — only use this when there's a real reason to act now.

---

## Variant 1 — Countdown Reminder

Best when: the deadline is approaching and you want to recap the value and handle final objections.

**Framework:**

1. Time reminder — state clearly how much time is left
2. Recap the key benefits of the offer
3. Address one common final objection
4. Name what they'll miss if they don't act
5. Clear, time-specific CTA

**Example:**

Only 24 hours left to join Transform Your Life at the beta price.

Here's what you get: weekly group coaching to keep you accountable, personalised action plans, a community of people working toward the same thing, and lifetime access to all the materials.

Worried you won't have the time? The programme is built for busy people — bite-sized daily steps and flexible session times. You're not signing up for more overwhelm, you're signing up for a structure that makes things easier.

After tomorrow, the price goes up by 50% and my personal feedback element closes. If you've been on the fence, this is the window.

Secure your spot before midnight: [LINK]

## Variant 2 — Last Chance With Bonus

Best when: the deadline is approaching AND you want to add an exclusive incentive for those who act now.

**Framework:**

1. Announce the last chance opportunity
2. Briefly recap the main offer
3. Introduce an exclusive last-minute bonus for those who act now
4. Explain why this is genuinely the final opportunity
5. Clear, time-specific CTA

**Example:**

Last chance — the special workshop attendee offer for [Programme Name] closes tonight.

Quick recap of what you get: [key features and benefits of the main offer].

For anyone who joins before midnight, I'm adding [exclusive bonus] — [what it is and why it's valuable]. This isn't something I'm including in the standard offer, so it's only available if you join now.

The price goes up at midnight and this bonus disappears with it.

Ready to get started? [LINK]

---

## Writing Notes

- State the deadline specifically — "midnight tonight" not "soon"
- The recap should be brief — they've seen the offer already
- For Variant 2, the bonus must be genuinely useful, not filler
- No apologetic language around urgency — state it plainly and confidently
- One CTA only, placed at the end
