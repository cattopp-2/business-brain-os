# Framework: Offer Post — Ready-to-Buy Segment

Speaks directly to people with a specific, urgent problem they want solved right now. Sets up a scenario they recognise, offers a solution that matches them exactly, and motivates immediate action.

Use when you want to speak to the segment of your audience who already knows they have the problem and are actively looking for a solution.

## The Framework

Are you / Is there [the outcome they want or the problem they're dealing with]?

[What's getting in their way?]

I can help you [the solution they want] without [the thing they don't want to do or give up].

We do this through:
- [Feature/benefit]
- [Feature/benefit]
- [Feature/benefit]

[CTA with a reason to take action now]

## Writing Notes

- The opening question should be ultra-specific — not "struggling with your business?" but "trying to fill your next cohort without running ads?"
- The "without" line removes a key objection before it's raised
- Feature/benefit bullets should be specific, not generic
- The urgency in the CTA should be real — a deadline, limited spots, or season-specific reason

## Example

Are you getting married in the next six months?

The stress of wedding planning, lack of time, and conflicting advice are making it hard to focus on being the healthiest, most confident version of yourself before the day.

I can help you feel brilliant in your dress without crash diets or spending hours at the gym.

We do this through:

- A personalised fitness plan built around your actual schedule and specific goals — not a generic programme
- Nutrition guidance that gives you energy and glowing skin without cutting out everything you enjoy
- Weekly check-ins timed around fittings and milestones, so progress is always visible
