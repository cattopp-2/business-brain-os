# Framework: Direct Offer — Long Form

A detailed offer post that works like a mini sales page. Organises all the relevant details in a way that builds interest, establishes credibility, and motivates action. Good for complex or higher-ticket offers where the audience needs more context.

## The Framework

1. Strong hook
2. Show you understand the problem
3. Introduce the solution
4. Reveal the offer
5. Explain how it works
6. Demonstrate proof (result or testimonial)
7. CTA
8. Add urgency if genuine

## Writing Notes

- The hook should open mid-scene or name the frustration directly — not a question
- "Explain how it works" should be specific: what happens, in what order, what they get
- Proof should be a specific result, not a vague testimonial
- Urgency only if real — don't manufacture it
