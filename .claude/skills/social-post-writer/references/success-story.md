# Framework: Success Story

Leads with a concrete client achievement to immediately capture attention and establish credibility. Shares the journey and specific strategies, then links to the offer.

Works well for building trust through real results. Balances inspiration with practical takeaways.

## The Framework

1. Lead with the specific outcome or result
2. Set the context — brief background on where they started
3. Share 2-3 key moments or strategies that made the difference
4. Link to the offer that helps others achieve similar results
5. Clear CTA

## Writing Notes

- Always attribute success to the client — Cathy's role is building the system or support
- Name specific results where possible (timeframes, numbers, concrete changes)
- Keep the strategy section practical — not vague inspiration
- The CTA should feel like a natural next step, not a pivot

## Example

One of my clients landed a £50,000 contract using a LinkedIn strategy we built together — after months of getting zero results from social media.

When James first came to me, he was posting consistently but nothing was landing. He'd tried the standard advice — motivational quotes, daily posting, commenting on trending content — but it never led to client conversations.

The shift came when we stopped trying to reach everyone and focused on building real relationships with specific people. We refined his profile to speak directly to CEOs of manufacturing companies, then developed a three-step engagement system: the right connections, meaningful interactions, and a clear process for moving conversations offline.

Within six weeks he had his first serious enquiry. By month three, he'd signed his biggest contract yet.

The difference wasn't working harder on LinkedIn. It was being deliberately strategic rather than just visible.
