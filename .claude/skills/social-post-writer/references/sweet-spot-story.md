# Framework: The Sweet Spot Story

Sits in the overlap between your experience and your audience's struggle. You've lived a version of what they're going through — and you found a way through it. That shared territory is where connection and authority meet.

Different from the Epiphany framework (which is a full origin story) and the Breakthrough Moment (which is about a specific insight). The Sweet Spot Story is specifically about the intersection of your personal journey and your client's current problem.

## The Framework

1. Describe the ideal client's current situation — their challenges, frustrations, desires
2. Show you understand — introduce yourself and your relevant background, empathise with their struggles
3. Name the problem clearly — what's really causing the issue
4. Your journey — share a personal experience with a similar problem and how you overcame it
5. Transformation — what changed, what you discovered
6. Solution — introduce the offer as the path to the same outcome
7. CTA

## Writing Notes

- The opening should describe the client's situation, not Cathy's
- The empathy step is about relatability — "I've been there" carries weight only when it's specific
- The problem naming should go deeper than the surface frustration — name the root cause
- Keep the personal journey section focused — one specific thread, not a full history
- The transformation should be concrete — what changed, how you know it worked
