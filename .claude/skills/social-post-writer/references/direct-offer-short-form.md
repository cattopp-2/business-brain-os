# Framework: Direct Offer — Short Form

A short, punchy offer post. Works as a direct, conversational pitch — "if I offered to do X, would you take me up on it?" Strips everything back to the core value.

Good for warm audiences, re-promotion of an existing offer, or when you want something that gets to the point fast.

## The Framework

If I offered to [what you do]...

- where we [part 1 of the process]
- then [part 2 of the process]
- and [part 3 of the process]

Would you take me up on that?

If so, drop me a message and let's chat.

## Writing Notes

- Keep the three steps specific and outcome-focused — not vague features
- The "would you take me up on that?" line is a soft, conversational CTA — keep the tone light
- This works best when the audience already has some awareness of Cathy

## Example

If I offered to help you get potential clients reaching out to you consistently...

- where we look at your entire process and distil your expertise into a clear offer framework that's easy to talk about
- then develop a message so people immediately understand what they get from working with you
- and set up a content system that builds your business organically without eating your week

Would you take me up on that?

If so, drop me a message and let's have a chat.
