# Framework: Signature Offer

For offers that are already selling and need more clients. Provides a structured approach that builds interest, provides the necessary details, and encourages action. More complete than the short-form — names the audience, leads with a result, explains the offer in detail.

## The Framework

1. Call out the audience
2. Lead with a result (testimonial or specific outcome)
3. Explain what the offer is in detail — what happens, what they get
4. Dial in the ideal client further (who it's for and who it isn't)
5. Share the investment
6. Clear CTA

## Writing Notes

- The result upfront should be specific — numbers, timeframes, concrete changes
- "Explain the offer in detail" means the actual mechanics — not vague benefits
- The ideal client section helps the right people self-qualify and wrong fits opt out
- Price and CTA at the end, after value is established
