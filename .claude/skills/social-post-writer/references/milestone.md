# Framework: Milestone

Celebrates a specific achievement while unpacking the journey and learnings that made it possible. Combines celebration with education — shows what's possible while giving practical steps forward. Humanises the business journey while building authority.

## The Framework

1. Lead with the specific milestone or achievement
2. Share 2-3 key lessons from the journey
3. Offer practical takeaways the audience can use
4. Link to your offer that helps others achieve similar milestones
5. Clear CTA

## Writing Notes

- Be specific about the milestone — numbers, timeframes, outcomes
- The lessons should be real and honest, including things that didn't go smoothly
- Practical takeaways are what elevate this beyond a humble brag — give real value
- Keep the offer mention brief and natural, not a hard pivot

## Example

Just hit a milestone this month — my clients have collectively paid off £1 million in debt while building emergency funds averaging £5,000 each.

The breakthrough that made this possible wasn't a stricter budget. It was three principles that changed how my clients think about money entirely.

Values-Based Spending: aligning money decisions with what actually matters to you, rather than following rigid rules that have nothing to do with your life. Budget-Based Goal Setting: setting financial targets based on your lifestyle vision, not arbitrary numbers. And Guilt-Free Progress: measuring success by movement, not perfection.

These things sound simple. They're surprisingly hard to apply without someone holding the mirror up.
