# Learnings — social-post-writer
Captured feedback from previous sessions. Read this before running the skill.

---

### 2026-05-15
Don't open with "If you're an [identity label]..." or describe who the reader is back to them ("You're an experienced business owner"). Speak to them directly at all times — address what they're experiencing, not who they are. The reader should feel you're talking to them, not sizing them up.

Don't use "that's the gap" or "that's the gap X fills" — state what the solution does directly instead.

Always speak TO the reader, not about them. "You're busy with clients" not "You've got a full client load." "But when your marketing" not "But when marketing." Every sentence should feel addressed to them, not describing them from the outside.

Never use "client roster."

When Cathy describes her own habits or systems she still uses, show personality — e.g. "(still rocking that system!)" not a dry statement.
