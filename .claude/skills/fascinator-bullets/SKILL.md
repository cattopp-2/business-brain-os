# Fascinator Bullets Skill

## What This Skill Does

Generates curiosity-driven, benefit-focused bullet points using 50 proven fill-in-the-blank frameworks. These bullets are designed to tease a specific result or secret without revealing it fully — compelling the reader to take action to find out more.

Use them for:
- Sales pages and landing pages
- Lead magnet opt-in pages
- Email sequences
- Course or programme sales copy
- Book or report promotion
- Webinar or event registration pages

---

## Step One: Select and Apply the Right Frameworks

Use the 50 frameworks below. **Do not use all 50 every time.** Select the frameworks that best fit:
- The type of offer (a book needs page-number frameworks; a video course does not)
- The tone the user wants
- The topics available in the offer

Aim for variety — mix curiosity-driven frameworks with specific-result frameworks, warning-style with discovery-style, and personal-story with expert-authority.

## Step Two: Write the Bullets

Write each bullet as a complete, polished sentence. Follow these rules:

**Do:**
- Be specific — vague bullets don't create curiosity. Name a number, a timeframe, a person type, a method.
- Tease the benefit without giving away the answer.
- Match the voice to the offer (bold and punchy for strong sales copy; warmer and grounded for coaching or wellness).
- Vary the structure across the set — don't use the same framework more than twice in a row.
- Use "you" to speak directly to the reader.

**Do not:**
- Use hollow filler phrases like "amazing secrets" or "life-changing results" with no specificity behind them.
- Overuse exclamation marks — pick the moments where they add emphasis rather than scattering them everywhere.
- Write bullets that could apply to any offer in any niche — make them specific to this offer and this reader.
- Start multiple bullets in a row with the same word or structure.

---

## Step Three: Present and Refine

1. Present the bullets in a clean numbered list.
2. Briefly note which frameworks were used and why, if helpful.
3. Offer to swap any frameworks, adjust tone, or write more bullets for specific sections of the offer.
4. If the user wants bullets for a specific section (e.g. "only benefits about decision fatigue"), refocus accordingly.

---

## Output Format

Present bullets as a plain numbered list, one bullet per line, ready to copy and paste into a document or page. No headers needed within the bullet list itself unless the user has asked for bullets grouped by theme.

Example output style:

```
1. You'll discover my 3-step Thought Decluttering process — this is the truly lazy way to clear your mental backlog in under 4 minutes.

2. Revealed: Why making a longer to-do list will NEVER reduce your overwhelm — and what you need to do instead.

3. WARNING: These 5 common decision-making mistakes will absolutely drain your mental energy by midday. Here's what to do instead to stay sharp all afternoon.
```
