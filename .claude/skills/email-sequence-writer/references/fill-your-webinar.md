# Email Sequence: Fill Your Webinar
# 6 Invite Emails (sent to warm list to drive registrations)

Use this framework to write a 6-email sequence sent to your existing list to invite them to
register for an upcoming webinar or masterclass.

Reference the PS Statements swipe file (ps-statements-swipe-file.md) for PS lines.

---

## Email 1 — Share Results
Subject: [creative subject line that evokes curiosity]

Aim: Share a case study or testimonial, connect it to the webinar.

Structure:
- Share a case study or testimonial, and let them know you're running an event to help them get the same result
- Introduce your webinar and share the outcomes they can expect if they join
- Make it clear any advantages/bonuses if they join live (vs catching the replay)
- Include the webinar details (date, time, link)
- Describe the webinar outcomes (3 bullet points)
- Include a powerful CTA using FOMO
- Sign off
- PS — use a relevant PS from the swipe file

## Email 2 — Share a Story
Subject: [creative subject line that evokes curiosity]

## Email 6 — Last Chance
Subject: [creative subject line that evokes curiosity]

Aim: Urgency. Registration is closing.

Structure:
- Open with a reminder that time is running out
- Explain exactly what's going away/changing if they don't join before the deadline
