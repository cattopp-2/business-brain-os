# Email Sequence: Freebie to Low Ticket Offer
# 6-Part Welcome Sequence

Use this framework to write a 6-email nurture sequence for someone who has downloaded a freebie,
warming them up to purchase a low-ticket paid offer.

Reference the PS Statements swipe file (ps-statements-swipe-file.md) for PS lines.

---

## Email 1 — Send immediately
Subject: Welcome! [NAME OF FREEBIE]

Structure:
- Say hi
- Give them what they signed up for (link to freebie)
- Introduce yourself briefly
- Let them know what to expect from you
- Use a relevant PS from the PS Statements swipe file
- Sign off

---

## Email 2 — Send after 6 hours
Subject: Choose one of:
- Did you get it?
- Just checking in…

Structure:
- A quick check-in to see if they got the download
- Ask them to reply
- Sign off
- PS

---

## Email 3 — Send 24–48 hours later

Structure:
- Remind them of the benefit from the freebie that links to their goals and desires
- Show how what's inside the freebie will deliver the outcomes they desire
- Use creative language and ways of articulating the freebie that haven't been used in previous emails
- Sign off
- PS — a short PS that introduces the paid product, focusing on the outcome or transformation

---

## Email 4 — Send 24–48 hours later

Structure:
- Share a story about why you started your business
- Introduce your low-ticket product
- Show how your low-ticket product will deliver the outcome they desire, focusing on the benefits
- Include a powerful call to action to buy now, using FOMO
- Use creative language and ways of articulating the low-ticket product not used in previous emails
- Sign off
- PS

---

## Email 5 — Send 24–48 hours later

Structure:
- Share a testimonial that shows how the paid product delivers benefits and outcomes
- Show why current solutions are not working
- Introduce your solution
- Show how your solution will deliver the outcomes they desire
- Use creative language not used in previous emails
- Include a powerful call to action
- Sign off
- PS

---

## Email 6 — Send 24–48 hours later

Structure:
- Write a hook
- Include details of the offer (the nuts and bolts — what happens / what they get)
- Include a strong CTA as the final paragraph
- Sign off
- PS
