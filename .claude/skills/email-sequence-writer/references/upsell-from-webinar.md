# Email Sequence: Upsell from Webinar or Masterclass
# 5-Email Post-Event Sales Sequence

Use this framework to write the follow-up email sequence after a webinar, selling a related offer
to attendees. Only include Email 5 if there is a genuine deadline.

---

## Email 1 — Workshop Recording & Resources (send same day or next morning)
Subject: Your workshop recording is ready

Hey [Name],

Thank you for joining me for [Workshop Name].

Here's everything I promised you:
- Workshop replay [LINK]
- [Include any additional resources]

Take 5 minutes today to [quick action step]. This will help you [quick win].

Cheers,
[Name]

PS — If you're ready to dive deeper into [topic], I've created a special offer exclusively for workshop attendees. More details coming tomorrow...

---

## Email 2 — The Offer Introduction (send next day)
Subject: Workshop attendees only: [Offer Name]

Hey [Name],

During the workshop, we covered [key topic].

Several people asked about getting more support with [specific challenge].

So I've created something special for workshop attendees:

[Offer details — what it is, how it works]

Here's what makes this different:
[3–4 unique elements of your offer]

[Include any deadlines or urgency if relevant]

Want to chat about whether this is right for you? Hit reply and let's talk.

Cheers,
[Name]

---

## Email 3 — Case Study / Social Proof
Subject: From workshop attendee to [specific result]

Hey [Name],

[Client name] attended this workshop last month.

[Share their story — focused on implementation and results]

The difference? They took action on what they learned, and got support inside [program name].

Right now you can [details of offer] and get:
[Bullet points of key features/benefits]

[Include any deadlines or urgency if relevant]

Ready to get started? [LINK]

Cheers,
[Name]

---

## Email 4 — Handle Main Objection
Subject: Worried about [main objection]?

Hey [Name],

The most common question I get about [offer] is [objection].

Here's the reality:
[2–3 paragraphs addressing the objection with examples/proof]

This is why inside [offer name] I've included:
[Features that specifically address this objection]

There's still time to [details of the offer].

But only until [deadline].

Ready to join? [LINK]

Cheers,
[Name]

---

## Email 5 — Last Call (ONLY include if there is a genuine deadline)
Subject: Last chance: Workshop attendee offer expires today

Hey [Name],

Quick reminder — the special workshop attendee offer for [program name] expires today.

To recap:
- What they get
- Main benefits
- Unique features
- Price
- Deadline

[One paragraph of encouragement]

Ready to get started? [LINK]

Cheers,
[Name]

PS — The price goes up at midnight. Lock in your spot now: [LINK]
