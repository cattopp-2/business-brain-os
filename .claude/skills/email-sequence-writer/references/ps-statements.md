# PS Statements — Swipe File

Choose the template most relevant to the email's context and offer. Rewrite in the sender's voice — these are starting points, not copy-paste.

---

**PS 1 — Facebook group / community invite**
PS If you haven't already, don't forget to join my FREE [community name]. Inside I [overview of the value you add].

**PS 2 — Quick survey**
PS I'd love to find out more about YOU! If you've got 30 seconds, here's a quick survey I'd appreciate you filling out. It'll help me know how to help you better going forward. [LINK]

**PS 3 — Reply invitation**
PS Got any questions? Hit reply and I'll sort it out for you.

**PS 4 — Reminder to use the freebie**
PS Are you digging into [Name of Freebie]? Everything you need is in there to [the outcome you're promising]. I'll explain more in the coming days.

**PS 5 — Book a call (soft)**
PS Would you like to have a chat about whether working with me is a good fit for you right now? No obligation — book a time here. [LINK]

**PS 6 — Book a call (urgency)**
PS I've only got a couple of spots left this month. Fill out the application form and I'll get back to you within 48 hours. [LINK]

**PS 7 — Referral**
PS If you know someone who would benefit from [name of training or offer], send them over to register. [LINK]

**PS 8 — Upcoming value teaser**
PS As promised, I'll be dropping more ideas on [the solution you provide] in your inbox over the next few days.
