# About Me

Save any existing material about yourself or your business here before running `setup-user-profile`.

Claude will read these files and extract what it needs — so you don't have to answer every question from scratch.

**What to save here:**

- Your website About page (copy and paste as a .md or .txt file)
- A bio you use on social media or speaker profiles
- A brand guide or brand story document
- Your LinkedIn About section
- Notes you've written about your business, your mission, or who you are
- A past proposal or pitch deck intro

**File format:** Any plain text format works — `.md`, `.txt`, or paste directly into a new file. Name the file something descriptive (e.g. `website-about-page.md`, `linkedin-bio.md`).

You don't need everything listed above — even one good piece of writing gives Claude enough to work with. The more you add, the more accurate your USER.md will be.
